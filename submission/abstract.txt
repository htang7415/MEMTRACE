MEMTRACE is a validity-first benchmark for evaluating persistent-memory risk in tool-using agents. It separates current-turn retrieval-context violations, poisoned-memory admission, trigger-time memory retrieval, unsafe proposal, policy-check blocking, unsafe execution, and execution-format failure. The v1.0 audited pilot uses 324 main traces for one exact actor/backend pair, mlx-community/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-4bit with MLX writer/planner backends, plus a separate 72-trace forced-memory calibration packet. The main result shows nonzero one-shot unsafe execution and S1 poisoned-memory admission, but zero delayed stateful unsafe execution under the declared protocol; the calibration demonstrates that the benchmark and scorer detect delayed memory-mediated unsafe execution when poisoned memory is forced into the trigger context.
